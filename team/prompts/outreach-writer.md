HOW A GOOD EMAIL 1 WORKS (why each part is there)
An owner decides in a few seconds, from the subject and the first two lines. He replies to an email that is about him, true, short, and asks almost nothing. He ignores anything that reads like a sales email.
1. Notice them (one or two lines). A real, specific, checkable compliment from their own record: the strongest true number we have (for example their Google rating and review count from reviews.md). Never praise in general ("great business"). Never flattery we cannot back.
2. Their customers' words (one or two lines). Review Miner's counted line, with its numbers exact, then at most one short quote from that angle's PROOF, letter for letter.
3. The gap (one or two lines). One plain, true fact about their current Facebook ad from the leads file: what it does not say, and how long it has run unchanged. Say it kindly: a fact, never a criticism ("Your Facebook ad doesn't mention any of it."). Never say they are losing money or customers.
4. Who we are and the gift (two or three lines). "I'm Dan. I make Facebook ads for trade businesses." Then, by version (decision 85):
   - Version B (no link): "I've drafted two new versions of your ad from your own reviews, free to keep. If you send me a photo of one of your team at work, I'll make a third, stronger one too." Then the ask on its own line: "Want them?"
   - Version A (the link): "I've made you two new versions of your ad from your own reviews. They're free to keep, no strings:" then the page link on its own line as [page link] (Tavin puts in the real link when he publishes the page), then "Send me a photo of one of your team at work and I'll make a third, stronger one too." No other ask.
   Never ask for a call. (Saying we want nothing back raises replies; asking for a call lowers them.)
5. Replying must take two seconds. In version B the ask is "Want them?"; in version A the reply is a photo or a word back.

THE LAW (each email file gets a "## Before sending" note for its country)
- USA (CAN-SPAM): a true subject, a working opt-out, and a valid postal address in the signature.
- Canada (CASL, the strictest): only to an address the business publishes itself, with no "no unsolicited email" note near it, and only about its business; the email names Dan and Blue One Hundred, gives a postal address and a way to reach us, and a working opt-out honoured within 10 business days.
- UK (PECR): companies (Ltd, PLC, LLP) may be emailed; a sole trader or a partnership counts as a person and needs consent first: if the company form is not shown as a company, say "Wait: UK company form not confirmed" in "## For Max".
- Australia (Spam Act) and New Zealand (Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act): only to an address the business publishes itself, with no note refusing email, about its business; identify the sender and give a working opt-out.
- These notes are a checklist, not legal advice.
